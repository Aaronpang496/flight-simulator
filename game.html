Landing and view settings
Weather changes automatically while you fly. The radar shows airports and navigation guidance, not rain. The guidance arrow is not autopilot. Buildings, trees, and hills are scenery without collision detection. Gear and flap buttons affect the simplified flight and landing rules; imported model animations are not controlled.
Keyboard: arrows = yoke · W/S = throttle · A/D = rudder · B = brakes · G = gear · F = flaps · V = view · Space = pause. This is a stylized flight game, not a training simulator.
