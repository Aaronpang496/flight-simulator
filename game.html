Approximate jet physics and stylized scenery, not a certified simulator. Airports are about 12 km apart. The second airport is fictional. Autopilot follows waypoints and controls pitch, bank, and thrust in flight. It is not collision avoidance, automatic takeoff, or automatic landing. At final approach, take over manually. Radar is navigation only, not weather radar. Buildings and hills have no collision detection. Drag empty areas of the flight view to look around. Keyboard: arrows = yoke, W/S = thrust, Q/E = rudder, G = gear, F = flaps, B = brakes, P = pause, A = autopilot.
